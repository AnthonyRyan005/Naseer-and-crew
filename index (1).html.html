How to play

  • You play one hero. The rest of the crew follows you and helps out.
  • Tap a friend's face at the top to call for help. Frankie smashes, Spencer hacks, Jaxxs pranks, James pulls you up, Ollie kicks, and Naseer slows time.
  • Jump on Glitch Bots to bop them, throw your object at them, or use your special move. Pick what each hero throws on the "Pick your hero" screen.
  • Teachers can't be fought. Stand still in an open locker to hide, sneak behind them, or get Jaxxs to distract them.
  • Bopping bots and teaming up fills the crew meter. When it's full, use Crew Charge!
