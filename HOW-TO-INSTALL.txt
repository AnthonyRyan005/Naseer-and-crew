Naseer and the Crew: installing on iPad and iPhone

1. Put this whole folder online. One easy way is Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop).
   Sign in or create a free account, then drag this folder onto the page.
   Netlify gives you a web link for the game.
2. On the iPad or iPhone, open that link in Safari.
3. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Leave "Open as Web App" on, then tap Add.
4. Open Naseer & Crew from the Home Screen. It plays full screen and works offline after the first visit.

Stars and looks are saved on each device separately.
To update the game later, drag the new folder onto your Netlify project's deploys page.
