How to play

  • You play one hero. The rest of the crew follows you and helps out.
  • Tap a friend's face at the top to call for help. Frankie smashes, Spencer hacks, Jaxxs pranks, James pulls you up, Ollie kicks, Ben freezes baddies with a spell, Ryan bumps baddies out of the way, and Naseer slows time.
  • Jump on Glitch Bots to bop them, throw your object at them, or use your special move. Pick what each hero throws on the "Pick your hero" screen.
  • Before each level, pick your hero and up to 3 friends. Any mix works: if a job needs a friend who isn't in your crew, they pop in to help.
  • If a baddie gets you, a friend is sent home and you lose a heart. Lose all 4 hearts and you start the level again.
  • Every level ends with a boss battle: Bully Invaders, Bully Pong, Bully Maze or the Schoolyard Showdown. Lose one and you can try it again straight away.
  • Tap Can you beat Paul at Poo Pong? on the main menu to play Poo Pong on its own. Play against Paul by yourself, or with 2 players each play your hero or Paul. Beat Paul and he goes up a level.
  • Watch out for Crusher the bully! Stop him with special moves before a grown-up comes, or take him on one on one in an Annoy-Off. Annoy him until his keeping cool bar hits 100%. If yours gets there first, everyone gets sent home.
  • If a grown-up catches you, they march a friend home. Win them back in a promise battle at the end of a level.
  • Teachers can't be fought. Stand still in an open locker to hide, sneak behind them, or get Jaxxs to distract them.
  • Bopping bots and teaming up fills the crew meter. When it's full, use Crew Charge!
  • To play with a friend on another device, tap Play online. One of you hosts and gets a 4-letter code, and the other joins with it. Only someone with your code can join. If the connection drops, your friend's game gets back in by itself. If they closed the game, they tap Play online, then Rejoin. Pause the game to see how good the connection is.
